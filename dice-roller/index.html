Dice Roller

A simple and elegant dice rolling app for board games and RPGs

Mobile Android

About

Dice Roller is a clean, minimalist app designed for tabletop gaming enthusiasts. Whether you're playing Dungeons & Dragons, board games, or any game that requires dice, this app provides a quick and intuitive way to roll virtual dice.

Features

🎲

Multiple Dice Types

Support for D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, and D20

🔢

Roll Multiple Dice

Roll several dice at once with individual results

➕

Automatic Sum

Instantly see the total of all your rolls

📜

Roll History

Track previous rolls with timestamps

🎨

Clean Design

Minimalist interface for distraction-free rolling

🔒

Privacy First

No data collection, no ads
