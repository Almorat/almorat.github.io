Privacy Policy

Sudoku App

Overview

Sudoku ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information Collection

We do not collect any personal information. The App is designed to work entirely offline and does not require any user accounts, registration, or personal data.

Data Storage

All game data, including your progress, statistics, and saved games, is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: Only used to save your game progress locally on your device

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
